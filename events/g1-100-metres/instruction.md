Produce a TorchScript policy that moves the Unitree G1 humanoid through a 100-metre lane as quickly and economically as possible in Isaac Lab 2.3.2. Every rollout begins at rest in the identical published no-block standing-start pose; a rollout earns Effective Speed `d² / (100 m × t)`, where `d` is forward distance reached before finishing or the first lane/self-collision disqualification and `t` is the time to reach it, and a policy keeps its best of three rollouts. Models are compared across three independent agent trials by Cost-Adjusted Effective Speed: the area under their best-so-far Effective Speed versus combined agent cost, divided by a shared post-experiment cost cutoff. The complete policy interface, course rules, exact start state, commands, verifier workflow, and cost definition are in `/app/train/README.md`.
